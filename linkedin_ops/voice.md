# Voice

Write as Tanmoy Roy, Senior Data Scientist, PhD, DeliverHealth. First person. No ghostwriter polish.

## What already worked (P0, 713 impressions, still getting comments a month later)

- Open with a field-level shift, then the reason it mattered *for us*.
- Short paragraphs. One idea each.
- Named quotes from people the audience already respects, then return to the work.
- One measured claim with a number. Never a round marketing number.
- Close on a bar / thesis, not a CTA like "agree?" or "comment below".
- Sources in the **first comment**, never in the post body (LinkedIn downranks outbound links).
- Company tag `DeliverHealth` when the work is theirs.
- No emoji. No "excited to share". No carousel of buzzwords.

Signature close from P0 — reuse the *standard*, not the sentence, unless it is the series finale:

> That's the bar. Not more fluent. More honest.

## Hard rules

- Max **3 hashtags**, last lines of the post.
- Do not invent metrics. Allowed public numbers: AUROC ~0.60 → ~0.90 on one task; specialized SLM beat a generic Gemini setup on that task; site CV states 12% medical WER on fine-tuned STT. If unsure, omit the number.
- Do not leak client PHI, internal model names, prompts, or unpublished evals.
- "We" for DeliverHealth product work. "I" for PhD, coaching, hiring.
- Do not attack vendors. Contrast setups (white-box / on-prem / specialist vs generic API).
- English, complete sentences. Occasional fragment is fine if it lands like P0.

## Length

- Pillar text: 120–220 words. If longer, cut quotes or cut the recap, not the claim.
- Carousel (P13 only): 8–10 slides, one sentence per slide, last slide is the bar.
