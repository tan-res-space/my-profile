# Hashtag bank

Exactly **3** per post. Mix one niche + one industry + one stance. Never more than 3. Never #AI or #MachineLearning as the only tags.

## Core (rotate; these already reached HIM + healthtech on P0)

| Tag | Role | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| #HealthcareAI | industry | almost always on Slot B |
| #SmallLanguageModels | niche / series | SLM thesis posts |
| #AgenticAI | niche | conversion, routing, copilots |
| #ResponsibleAI | stance | eval, deferral, calibration |
| #OnPremiseAI | differentiator | data residency, white-box, single GPU |
| #SpeechAI | niche | ASR, endpoints, ambient notes |
| #ClinicalNLP | niche | coding, notes, RAG correction |

## Allowed secondary (at most one per post)

#MedASR #HealthTech #MLOps #DataScience #SpeechRecognition

## Banned

#hiring #motivation #AI #ChatGPT #LLM as a lone tag #OpenToWork hashtag stuffing (>3)

P0 used six tags and still worked. Do not copy that. Three is the 2026 spam-threshold most India growth guides cite; extra tags after three add little and look like 2019 creator advice.
