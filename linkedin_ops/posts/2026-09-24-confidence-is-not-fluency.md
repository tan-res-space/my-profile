# Body (paste into LinkedIn)

A HIM director does not care that your model is “state of the art.”

They care whether it knows when to shut up.

Frontier APIs will give you a fluent note and a verbal “I’m sure.” That is not a score. You cannot plot it. You cannot set a deferral threshold. You cannot show a compliance officer a ROC curve.

White-box SLMs give you token probabilities and hidden states. Cheap enough to score several candidates per case instead of one confident guess. That is why we run the specialist on a single GPU, on our side of the network. Not because small is fashionable. Because inspection is a product requirement in clinical NLP.

A comment on my last post put it cleanly: your data is the moat, and SLMs are how you use it without shipping it out.

The test I use now: if you cannot draw a calibration curve, you do not have a confidence score. You have a vibe.
