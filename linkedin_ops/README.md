# LinkedIn operations

Personal content system for Tanmoy Roy. Not part of the Jekyll site.

## Cadence

- **Slot A** — Wednesday 08:00 IST. India / APAC practitioners. MagicPost (220,220 India posts, May 2026): Wednesday 08:00 scores 96/100 and beats the 10:00 IST volume spike.
- **Slot B** — Thursday 19:00 IST (09:30 ET). US healthcare / HIM / healthtech, plus India evening. Buffer (4.8M posts, Sep 2026) now peaks 15:00–20:00 local; this slot is Thursday evening IST and Thursday morning US.
- **2 posts/week** for 8 weeks (23 Sep–13 Nov 2026). Buffer: 2–5/week is where distribution starts compounding vs weekly. Quality over a third midweek post until P8 review.
- **Daily:** 3 substantive comments on other people’s posts in healthcare AI, SLM/agents, HIM, speech. This is not optional. LinkedIn samples a small first audience; comment graph is how a 853-follower account gets out of the 713-impression band.
- **Golden window:** be online 60–90 minutes after each publish. Reply to every comment. PostPika / creator guides still treat early comment velocity as the expansion signal; LinkedIn has not published a formal “golden hour,” but ignoring comments in the first hour is the usual way a good post dies.

## Do not

- Put outbound links in the post body.
- Use more than 3 hashtags.
- Invent eval numbers.
- Auto-post through unofficial LinkedIn scrapers. Native LinkedIn scheduler or paste-at-slot. The LinkedIn posting API is not in this repo.
